🎓 Admission Open to All Units

De Prince International School proudly accepts admissions into all units of the school. Whether you are seeking enrollment for your toddler in our Creche, your child in Primary, or your teenager in Junior or Senior Secondary, we welcome you.

Our admission process is simple, transparent, and designed to place every student in the class best suited for their academic growth. We also offer JAMB and WAEC registration for eligible students preparing for tertiary education.
