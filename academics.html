📖 Academic Excellence at De Prince

At De Prince International School, we maintain the highest academic standards, blending Nigerian and international curricula to provide a comprehensive education that meets global benchmarks. Our students consistently rank among the top performers in national and regional examinations.

Our teaching methodology emphasizes critical thinking, problem-solving, creativity, and digital literacy. With small class sizes, every student receives individual attention from our highly qualified educators who are passionate about bringing out the best in every learner.
